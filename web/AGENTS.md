# Prototype Instructions

Run the local server yourself and open the preview in the browser available to this environment. Do not give the user server-start instructions when you can run it.

Before making substantial visual changes, use the Product Design plugin's `get-context` skill when the visual source is unclear or no longer matches the current goal. When the user gives durable prototype-specific design feedback, preferences, or decisions, record them in `AGENTS.md`.

When implementing from a selected generated mock, treat that image as the source of truth for layout, component anatomy, density, spacing, color, typography, visible content, and hierarchy.

## Durable product requirements

- Every completed code update must increment the visible `uiVersion` in `src/App.jsx`. The handoff must explicitly state which containers need rebuilding (`web`, `api`, or both) and whether an automatic database migration is included.
- Every deployment handoff must list all changed files that must be synchronized to the NAS. When dependencies change, call out the relevant package manifest and lockfile separately (for example `web/package.json` and `web/pnpm-lock.yaml`) before giving rebuild commands; never assume the user deploys with a full Git checkout.
- The ERP is multilingual by design. All new user-facing copy must be added through `src/i18n.jsx`; do not hardcode new UI strings in business components.
- Initial supported locales are Simplified Chinese (`zh-CN`), English (`en`), and Hungarian (`hu`). Missing keys intentionally fall back to Simplified Chinese.
- During hardcoded-copy audits, preserve Chinese import aliases, persisted status identifiers, and user-entered business data. Translate their rendered labels instead of rewriting compatibility values or stored records.
- Overselling is controlled by the administrator's basic-information setting and defaults to enabled. When enabled inventory may be negative; when disabled the backend must prevent every sales action that would reduce stock below zero.
- The left navigation must not contain the obsolete “Control” group. Basic information settings are real persisted business configuration covering company details, HUF exchange rates, default sales currency, overselling, low-stock threshold, default warehouse and sales/purchase document prefixes.
- The obsolete “App / Store” navigation group and its child pages must not appear in the left navigation.
- The Sales menu includes a warehouse picking-list page for administrators and warehouse staff. The menu and query page are named “配货单列表”, while the opened print page, browser-style tab, and printed sheet heading are named “配货单”. It is strictly read-only, uses a restricted API, supports date filtering, order detail and printing, and must never expose current stock, currency, prices, totals, payments, sales statuses or editing controls.
- Delivery-note printing has a browser-persisted field selector for SKU, barcode, product name, product name 2, current stock, quantity, unit price and line total. Picking-list printing may reuse it but must always exclude current stock and all financial fields.
- Every product has a persistent warehouse and an optional storage location. Existing products are initialized to the configured default warehouse, and new/manual or imported products inherit it. The inventory list has no create-product action. Its row edit dialog can change stock quantity, box size, unit, warehouse and storage location under the dedicated `inventory.edit` permission; stock changes must create immutable `inventory_adjustment` movements for the delta. `inventory.edit` is not granted to any non-admin role by default and is configurable by administrators. The warehouse role defaults only to `inventory.locations`, which opens the same row action in storage-location-only mode and must not permit editing warehouse, quantity, box size, or unit. Inventory columns show warehouse immediately before storage location. Picking-list details and printouts may show storage location immediately after product name; delivery notes must not show it.
- The supported business currencies are fixed to HUF, EUR, and USD. Every product has an independent sales price in each currency, and sales flows must preserve the selected currency.
- Sales settlement is ledger-based: every cash or bank-transfer receipt is stored as an immutable payment record. Paid and due amounts and the unpaid/partial/paid status are derived from those records; they must never be manually toggled.
- Finance > Transactions is an administrator/finance-only cash ledger. It combines actual sales receipts, sale returns, negative-sale refunds and manual income/expense entries; supports date filters; separates HUF, EUR and USD totals; and shows income, expense, net, cash and bank-transfer breakdowns without adding different currencies together.
- Every saved scan-sale can carry a delivery contact phone, vehicle plate, delivery address, delivery date and notes. These fields persist with the sale, but delivery-note and picking-note printouts must not display the order delivery address. Both printouts show the customer's company address immediately below the customer name instead.
- Main navigation uses browser-style page tabs. Opening a menu item preserves existing pages, tabs retain their local UI state while inactive, each business tab can be closed independently, and the dashboard tab remains fixed.
- All primary business list views use shared client-side pagination with exactly 10 records per page, including products, inventory, customers, purchases, sales, picking lists, invoice worklists, invoice management, audit logs and employee management. Search or filter result changes must reset or clamp pagination to a valid page. Detail, editing, print and import-preview tables are not paginated.
- The dashboard must not show the obsolete smart-purchasing, recommended-supplier, or owner-view buttons, nor the four-card seven-day ranking section (seven-day top quantity, amount, profit, or stock warning). Keep the summary KPIs, quick actions, recent-sales panel, and a real seven-day order-count trend supplied by the dashboard API; never render demo trend values. The Finance quick action opens the transaction ledger.
- The header has no notification bell because notifications are not implemented. Its clock must show the live Europe/Budapest date and time instead of a hardcoded placeholder.
- The sidebar footer shows the latest official MNB EUR/HUF and USD/HUF daily rates for reference only; it must never replace configured business rates or affect calculations. The MNB rates and visible UI version sit after the menu content and scroll with the sidebar instead of being fixed to the viewport.
- Product images are stored as files in the persistent uploads volume while PostgreSQL stores only randomized image URLs. Keep image uploads limited to JPG, PNG and WebP up to 5 MB, and never embed image binaries in the database.
- Sales staff and administrators can open an existing order for editing from the sales-list action column; editing must reuse the scan-sale screen and update the original order rather than create a duplicate.
- Customer master data includes a dedicated vehicle-plate field editable from the customer list. Scan-sale customer selection is a two-character-minimum typeahead over customer name, phone, tax number, the dedicated vehicle plate, and vehicle plates from that customer's non-deleted historical sales. Results close on outside pointer interaction or Escape and persist the selected customer ID, not the visible text.
- Customer-group member management opens a searchable customer table. Every row shows the customer's current group and an immediate Add to group or Remove from group action; do not use a checkbox-only bulk-selection interface.
- Customer-list columns always follow this fixed reading order: name, customer group, phone, vehicle plate, tax number, country, postal code, city, address, email, and notes. Keep notes last and wide enough for readable wrapping.
- New sales-order numbers use the configured prefix followed by the Europe/Budapest business date and a daily sequence, for example `Vs20260725-1`. Sequence allocation must be transactionally concurrency-safe, include soft-deleted sales when finding the next value, and never reuse a historical number.
- HUF sales pricing uses a positive product HUF price as-is. When the product HUF price is zero, scan sales, collector previews, and the authoritative create/update sales APIs must use the product EUR price multiplied by the configured EUR/HUF business rate and round to the nearest whole forint.
- The sales-order detail dialog shows each line's product thumbnail and current live inventory. This does not relax the warehouse picking-list restriction: picking APIs and picking dialogs must still omit current stock.
- The product list has user-selectable columns persisted in the browser. Selection only controls visibility; column order is always fixed as image, SKU, barcode, name, name 2, category, package size, box size, HUF/EUR/USD prices, inbound quantity, net sales quantity, supplier, cost, current inventory, creation time and unit. The default view stays intentionally compact.
- Product master data has separate positive-integer package size and box size fields. Package size means units per package, defaults to 1, and is always ordered immediately before box size in the product list, form, detail view, and spreadsheet import/export.
- Sales-order list columns are user-selectable and browser-persisted, while the action column remains fixed. Notes are a default visible column immediately after outstanding balance, wrap within a bounded width, and participate in keyword search. Administrators and sales users edit notes inline; Enter or blur saves and Escape cancels, while finance remains read-only. The picker must close when a pointer click occurs outside it.
- The product-list toolbar uses a real category selector populated from existing product categories. Category and keyword filters can be combined; the generic placeholder selector must not appear on the product page.
- Product categories are managed on a dedicated two-level tree page. A category has a primary name, a secondary-language name, an optional parent main category and an optional persistent image; category renames must keep product category assignments in sync.
- The category page supports Excel template download, import and export for both hierarchy levels. Each row names a main category and may name one child; repeated main-category rows represent multiple children. Imports may attach new children to an existing main category, validate the entire file first, and save all new categories in one transaction. Category images remain managed separately through the persistent image API.
- Product and category edit dialogs must allow an existing image to be deleted after confirmation. Deletion clears the stored URL and removes the corresponding file from the persistent uploads volume.
- The product list supports Excel import and export. Imports use a downloadable template, treat empty and whitespace-only cells as omitted, validate every row before saving, accept a positive, zero, or negative integer opening stock, reject duplicate SKU/barcode and unknown categories, and create products in one backend transaction. Omitted optional text becomes empty, box size defaults to 1, and omitted prices, cost and stock default to 0. Every nonzero imported stock value must create a matching immutable `product_import` inventory movement in that transaction. The first import column accepts one embedded JPG/PNG thumbnail per product row, matched by the image's top-left anchor; images are validated before product creation and uploaded through the persistent product-image API afterward. Exports contain product master data plus current stock and purchase/sales statistics.
- Product-list filtering uses linked main-category and subcategory selectors. Selecting only a main category includes that category and all direct children. Product create/edit forms must select an existing category and must not accept arbitrary category text.
- Product and inventory lists provide the same linked main-category/subcategory selectors plus working All, positive-stock, and zero-or-negative-stock filters. Category, stock-state, and keyword filtering can be combined. List keyword inputs are draft values: products, purchases, sales, picking lists, invoice worklists, invoice management, customers, and audit logs apply keywords only when Search is clicked or Enter is pressed; Refresh clears the applied keyword.
- In the product list, users with product-edit permission can click the category cell to open a linked main/subcategory picker and save the category immediately. The inventory-only view and users without edit permission keep the category cell read-only.
- The Customers navigation group contains only the customer list; the obsolete customer-accounts menu item must not appear.
- Számlázz.hu invoicing is server-side and uses only the NAS `SZAMLAZZ_AGENT_KEY` environment variable. Sales prices are net, invoice VAT is fixed at 27%, e-invoices are not emailed to buyers, and authenticated users can print or download the stored PDF. The invoice dialog shows the original sales-order net unit price as read-only and a separate editable invoice unit price. Whole-order factors are entered and displayed only as percentages, then converted to a decimal API factor. Invoice pricing must not modify the sale or inventory. An issued or attempted invoice locks the related sale against editing.
- Invoice > Create invoice is a date-filtered sales-order list that defaults to today, shows every sale and its invoice status, and disables issuing for already-issued or processing sales. Correction, standalone storno and reissue-after-storno live on Invoice > Invoice list. The sales menu's sales-order list exposes no invoice column or action. Issued invoices are immutable and never deleted; storno never automatically triggers reissue, and every document retains its number, PDF, customer snapshot, operator and parent link without changing sales, stock or payment ledgers.
- Számlázz.hu does not permit a corrected invoice chain to be stornoed. The UI must call that action zeroing, not voiding: it creates a zeroing correction and shows the chain as zeroed. Only an uncorrected invoice may be described as producing a genuine storno document.
- The invoice-list toolbar has separate selectors for document type, payment method, currency and invoice lifecycle status. Each selector works alone or in combination with the others and keyword search when the user presses Search; Refresh clears every selector and the keyword.
- Audit logs are visible only to administrators. Every authenticated POST, PUT, PATCH and DELETE request is recorded with business-timezone timestamp, user snapshot, module, action, target, outcome and sanitized request details. Passwords, tokens, Agent keys and binary image data must never be stored in audit details.
- Employee management is administrator-only. Administrators can create users, change roles, choose enabled/disabled status, reset another user's password, and delete any account except the current login. Password resets require at least eight characters, are scrypt-hashed by the API, and must remain redacted from audit details. Purchasing, warehouse, sales, and finance permissions are database-backed and configurable as separate page-access and operation permissions. The sidebar, business controls, and API must all enforce the same live permission set. Administrator access is always complete and cannot be reduced; employee management and audit logs cannot be delegated.
- Row deletion for products/inventory, categories, customers, purchases, sales and invoices is administrator-only in both the UI and API. Use auditable soft deletion. Purchase deletion reverses received stock; deletion of a stock-deducted sale restores only its remaining non-returned quantity; sales with any invoice record cannot be deleted. Deleting an invoice only hides its ERP record and must never call Számlázz.hu, remove the retained PDF, or permit a duplicate invoice for that sale.
- Product and inventory lists allow administrators to select rows individually or select the current page. A batch-delete action appears only while at least one row is selected, confirms the count, and deletes every selected product atomically through the administrator-only batch API.
- New purchase lines use a manual SKU search input instead of a product-name selector. Suggestions open only after at least three SKU characters and show every matching existing SKU; choosing one binds the product and its current purchase cost.
- A purchase receipt requires only an existing SKU and a positive integer quantity. Supplier and warehouse may be blank; a blank warehouse uses the system default. Unit cost is optional, counts as zero on that receipt when omitted, and must not overwrite the product's existing cost unless a value is supplied.
- Reports are administrator/finance-only and contain separate Sales, Finance and Customer tabs. Every report is date-filtered and analyzes exactly one selected currency so different currencies are never added together. Sales recognizes sales and returns on their occurrence dates; Finance is based only on actual cash-ledger events; Customer reporting combines period activity with current outstanding balances. Do not display historical profit until immutable line-level cost snapshots exist.
- Sales-report customer/product filters and the Customer-report customer filter are typeahead inputs: show contains-matches only after at least three characters and close results on outside click. Customer report does not expose customer-group or city filters, and Sales report does not show a salesperson ranking chart.
